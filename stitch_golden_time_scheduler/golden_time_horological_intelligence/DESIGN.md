## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the confluence of haute horlogerie and high-performance algorithmic intelligence. Built for high-net-worth executives, elite performers, and demanding practitioners, it treats personal time not as an open canvas, but as a finite, precious complication to be calibrated with mechanical precision.

The aesthetic philosophy draws heavily from dark-mode luxury, high-end Swiss chronograph interfaces, and bespoke executive command centers. It combines deep obsidian substrates with delicate golden filigree borders, refined atmospheric luminescences, and crystalline glass overlays. Every interaction must project calm authority, surgical calculation, and timeless prestige.

## Colors

The palette operates on calibrated contrast, anchored in pitch blacks and layered charcoal tones to simulate the polished interior of a luxury tourbillon watch case.

- **Obsidian Baseline (`#0A0A0A`, `#121212`)**: The foundational canvas; pure, deep, and non-distracting.
- **Charcoal Surfaces (`#18181B`, `#27272A`)**: Structured structural cards and contextual overlays that subtly rise above the baseline.
- **Metallic Gold (`#D4AF37`)**: The hero accent. Used for primary interactive triggers, hairline strokes, micro-indicators, and structural focus rings.
- **Radiant Sunburst (`#EAB308`)**: Reserved for peak habit milestones, algorithmic optimizations, and high-intensity focal states.
- **Crisp Stark White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Strictly utilized for primary editorial typography, headline data figures, and active time metrics.
- **Muted Platinum Gray (`#A1A1AA`)**: Delivers secondary visual weights, telemetry labels, axis markers, and inactive states without visual clutter.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances classical editorial prestige with scientific precision. 

- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display)**: Evokes the gravitas of bespoke print and high horology. Used for narrative titles, executive summaries, and key habit nomenclature. All serif headlines use refined negative tracking to maintain a modern, crisp presence.
- **Interface & Telemetry (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Ensures instant scanning of algorithmic schedules, machine learning probability scores, and chronological matrices. Numeric telemetry uses proportional or tabular figures to preserve rhythmic stability across dynamic data flows.
- **Labels & Micro-indicators**: Rendered in uppercase geometric sans with widened letter spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mirror luxury dial engravings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional, mathematically disciplined 12-column grid system designed around chrono-dashboards and predictive habit timelines.

- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: Max canvas boundary of `1440px`, flanked by `3rem` (`48px`) margins and `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters. Sidebars and chronometer panels lock into fixed 4-column modules, allowing the algorithmic core to occupy the remaining 8 columns.
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Spacing scales down with `2rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Metric modules compress into balanced dual-column groupings.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: Outer margins tighten to `1.25rem` (`20px`) with `1rem` gutters. Chronological timelines switch to a single unified vertical escapement track.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows, but rather on luminance, tonal layering, and metallic backlighting.

- **The Obsidian Canvas (`#0A0A0A`)**: Ground zero. Void of elevation.
- **Glassmorphic Substrates (`#18181B` at 70% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`)**: Suspended containers holding schedule blocks and habit trajectories.
- **Gold Filigree Tracing**: Outlined with `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)` to create crisp edges without aggressive visual mass.
- **Aura & Radiance**: Hover and active states project a soft radial luminescence (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`). High-priority AI recommendations receive a secondary sunburst rim glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 32px 0 rgba(234, 179, 8, 0.20)`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a restrained `Soft (1)` corner radius architecture, paying homage to faceted watch crystals and machined luxury chassis.

- **Primary Components**: `4px` (`0.25rem`) border radius on input surfaces, telemetry chips, and standard buttons to maintain an architectural, engineered quality.
- **Card Containers**: `8px` (`0.5rem`) for habit clusters and chronometer widgets.
- **Focal Elements**: Pill shapes (`9999px`) are strictly forbidden, except for circular status complication nodes and mechanical toggle pips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Chronograph Action)**: Solid metallic gold `#D4AF37` fill with obsidian `#0A0A0A` text (`fontWeight: 600`), bordered with a hairline stroke of `#EAB308`. Hover transitions activate an ambient golden bloom.
- **Secondary (Calibrated Frame)**: Pitch-black background, framed by `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`, filled with crisp white text. Hover states elevate background luminosity to `#18181B` with full gold border opacity.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Transparent background, text in muted gray `#A1A1AA`, shifting to pure white with gold under-pip on hover.

### Habit Cards & Glass Enclosures
- Surfaces use `#121212` with 80% opacity and `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- Bordered with an exact `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)` stroke.
- Inner padding holds to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`).
- Header displays habit title in `headline-sm` Playfair Display, accompanied by real-time adherence rates rendered in tabular Plus Jakarta Sans.

### Chips & Telemetry Tags
- Minimalist indicators displaying AI confidence metrics, habit cadence, and time blocks.
- Background: `#18181B`, border: `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`, text: uppercase `label-sm` in `#D4AF37`.

### Chrono Inputs & Fields
- Inset field tone: `#0F0F11` with a base border of `1px solid #27272A`.
- Active focus state transitions the border to `#D4AF37` backed by an ethereal glow (`0 0 12px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`).
- Typography within fields renders in sharp `#FFFFFF` sans-serif with placeholder text seated in `#52525B`.

### Checkboxes & Habit Completion Pips
- Square mechanical boxes (`18px x 18px`) with `2px` corners.
- Default: `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)` against dark charcoal.
- Completed: Snaps instantly to `#D4AF37` fill with an obsidian checkmark icon, triggering a brief micro-glow to celebrate task fulfillment.

### Specialized Complications
- **The Golden Orbit (AI Chronometer)**: A concentric radial dial tracking circadian rhythm alignment. Uses thin metallic gold strokes against dark backdrop dials.
- **Predictive Velocity Gauge**: Linear progression track featuring a high-precision gold gradient runner with diamond-cut division markers.
